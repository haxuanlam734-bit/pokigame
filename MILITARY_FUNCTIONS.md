# Military Base – Functional Buildings

Controls: walk close to a building and press **E** (or click the interaction button).

- COMMAND HQ: upgrade base, increase money multiplier, increase max money-machine capacity, repair base HP.
- BARRACKS: recruit guard troops; each recruit increases defense stat.
- MESS HALL: restore stamina and recover HP on cooldown.
- MEDICAL: fully heal player and repair fortress HP on cooldown.
- SUPPLY DEPOT: upgrade weapon tier, damage, and ammo capacity.
- FUEL FARM: sell fuel for money and improve production efficiency.
- MOTOR POOL: activate vehicle boost and increase movement speed.
- RESEARCH LAB: research weapon and production upgrades.
- VEHICLE WORKSHOP: upgrade vehicle level and stamina capacity.
- TRAINING GROUND: gain weapon XP and train toward weapon levels.
- SHOOTING RANGE: refill ammo and improve weapon damage.
- RADAR STATION: scan the active zombie population and level the radar.
- COMMS TOWER: claim a timed supply contract for money.

New saved progression includes HP/stamina/ammo, weapon tier/damage/XP, guards, base level, production multipliers, vehicle level and radar level.
